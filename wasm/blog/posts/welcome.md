Welcome to a blog that runs **entirely in your browser**. There is no
backend: every page you see was rendered from Markdown on your machine by
[MD4C](https://github.com/mity/md4c), compiled to WebAssembly.

## What you can do

- [x] Read the sample posts
- [ ] Click **New post** and write your own
- [ ] Edit a post and watch the live preview update as you type

Your posts are saved in this browser (IndexedDB), so they are still here
after a reload. To learn how the pieces fit together, read
[[How it works]]. To see what the renderer supports, open the
[[Markdown showcase]].

> [!TIP]
> Use **Reset to sample posts** in the footer to start over.
